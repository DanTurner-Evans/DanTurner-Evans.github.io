I am a Senior Fellow at the Institute for Progress, where I work on how AI and metascience can accelerate science. I run a competitive call for frontier AI-for-science proposals, write for policymakers and general audiences about how AI is changing research, and advocate for ideas that can strengthen the scientific enterprise.

I started in semiconductor physics, designing and building solar cells for my PhD at Caltech. I then moved to systems neuroscience, studying how neural networks in the brain give us our sense of direction, first at HHMI's Janelia Research Campus and then in my own lab at UC Santa Cruz. In 2024 I came to Washington as a AAAS Congressional AI Fellow on the Senate Appropriations Subcommittee on Energy and Water Development, where I worked on bringing AI into the Department of Energy and the national labs.

You can read about my research, browse my writing, teaching, and workshops, learn more about me, or email me.
